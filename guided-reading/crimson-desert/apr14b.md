# Phrase Analysis: 肥瘦比例

## Pinyin

féi shòu bǐ lì

## Pronunciation

[féi shòu bǐ lì](audio/minimax-tts_肥瘦比例_Chinese__Mandarin__News_Anchor_E46579A307.mp3)

## Meaning & Usage

**肥瘦比例** (féi shòu bǐ lì) = **"The ratio of fat to lean (meat)."** / **"The fat-to-lean ratio."**

This noun phrase is commonly used in **cooking, butchery, and food selection** to describe the **proportion of fat to lean meat** in a cut of meat or a meat mixture. It is a critical factor in determining the **flavor, texture, and cooking suitability** of meat — especially pork, beef, and lamb.

In Chinese culinary tradition, the **肥瘦比例** is not about maximizing leanness; rather, a **balanced or specifically targeted ratio** is often prized for different dishes. Too lean, and the meat becomes dry and tough; too fatty, and it becomes greasy.

## Literal Translation / Breakdown

**肥瘦比例** (féi shòu bǐ lì): **肥瘦** (fat and lean) + **比例** (ratio/proportion) = the proportion of fatty tissue to lean muscle in meat

**肥瘦** (féi shòu): **肥** (fat) + **瘦** (lean) = fat and lean (meat); the two components of meat quality

**比例** (bǐ lì): **比** (to compare/ratio) + **例** (proportion/example) = ratio, proportion, rate — the quantitative relationship between two or more things

**肥** (féi): Fat, fatty, obese; fertile, rich (of soil); lucrative, profitable Contains the **月** (meat/flesh) radical on the left — indicating its connection to bodily fat or meat.

**瘦** (shòu): Lean, thin, slim (of a person or meat); gaunt, emaciated Contains the **疒** (sickness/disease) radical, which in ancient usage conveyed the idea of being undernourished or wasting away.

**比** (bǐ): To compare, to contrast, ratio, proportion; next to, side by side The character originally depicted **two people standing side by side** for comparison.

**例** (lì): Example, precedent, proportion, ratio; regulation, custom Contains the **亻** (person) radical on the left.

# Idiom Analysis: 知己知彼，百战百胜

## Pinyin

zhī jǐ zhī bǐ, bǎi zhàn bǎi shèng

## Pronunciation

[zhī jǐ zhī bǐ, bǎi zhàn bǎi shèng](audio/minimax-tts_知己知彼_百战百胜_Chinese__Mandarin__News_Anchor_1A809D9337.mp3)

## Meaning & Usage

**知己知彼，百战百胜** (zhī jǐ zhī bǐ, bǎi zhàn bǎi shèng) = **"Know yourself, know your enemy, and you will win every battle."**

This famous idiom is a **military maxim** originating from **Sun Tzu's *The Art of War*** (*孙子兵法*, Sūn Zǐ Bīng Fǎ). It expresses the strategic principle that **understanding both your own strengths and weaknesses as well as those of your opponent** is the key to guaranteed success in any conflict or competition.

The phrase is widely used beyond military contexts today:

- **Business & competition**: Analyzing both your own company and your competitors before making a move.
- **Sports & gaming**: Studying both your own play style and your opponent's tendencies.
- **General strategy**: Any situation where preparation, self-awareness, and knowledge of the opposing side determine the outcome.

It is one of the most quoted lines from *The Art of War* and remains a cornerstone of strategic thinking.

## Literal Translation / Breakdown

**知己知彼，百战百胜** (zhī jǐ zhī bǐ, bǎi zhàn bǎi shèng): **知己** (know yourself) + **知彼** (know the other side / your enemy) + **百战** (a hundred battles) + **百胜** (a hundred victories) = if you understand both yourself and your opponent, you will be victorious in every engagement

**知己** (zhī jǐ): **知** (to know) + **己** (oneself) = to know oneself — to have honest self-awareness of one's own strengths, weaknesses, resources, and limitations

**知彼** (zhī bǐ): **知** (to know) + **彼** (the other side / that one) = to know the other side — to understand your opponent's capabilities, intentions, strategies, and weaknesses

**百战** (bǎi zhàn): **百** (hundred) + **战** (battle/war) = a hundred battles — meaning every battle, countless engagements

**百胜** (bǎi shèng): **百** (hundred) + **胜** (victory) = a hundred victories — meaning total, consistent victory

**知** (zhī): To know, to understand, to perceive; knowledge; to inform

**己** (jǐ): Oneself, one's own — the first-person reflexive pronoun

**彼** (bǐ): That, the other, the opposite side; he/him (classical) Contains the **彳** (step/march slowly) radical on the left.

**百** (bǎi): Hundred, many, all, every

**战** (zhàn): War, battle, fight, to fight, to wage war Contains the **戈** (spear/lance) radical on the right.

**胜** (shèng): To win, to defeat, victory, triumph; superior, better Contains the **月** (meat/flesh) radical on the left.

**八成是裂角的追随者干的** (bā chéng shì liè jiǎo de zhuī suí zhě gàn de) = **"It was most likely done by the followers of Split Horn."** / **"Chances are it's the Split Horn followers who did it."**

This sentence is a **speculative accusation** — the speaker is expressing a high degree of certainty about **who is responsible** for some event or action, but without definitive proof. The construction **八成是…干的** is a common spoken pattern for "It's probably/likely that… did it."

**裂角** (liè jiǎo — "Split Horn") appears to be a proper noun, likely the name of a **faction, leader, or group** within the game world.

## Literal Translation / Breakdown

**八成是裂角的追随者干的** (bā chéng shì liè jiǎo de zhuī suí zhě gàn de): **八成** (eight out of ten = very likely) + **是** (is/was) + **裂角的追随者** (the followers of Split Horn) + **干的** (did it / the ones who did it) = It's about 80% likely that the followers of Split Horn are the ones behind this

**八成** (bā chéng): **八** (eight) + **成** (tenth/part) = eight out of ten = probably, most likely, about 80% certain — a colloquial adverb expressing a strong guess

**是…干的** (shì… gàn de): **是** (it is/was) + **干的** (done by) = it was [someone] who did it — a cleft-like construction identifying the agent of an action

**裂角** (liè jiǎo): **裂** (to split/crack) + **角** (horn/angle) = Split Horn — a proper noun, likely the name of a faction, leader, or group

**追随者** (zhuī suí zhě): **追随** (to follow) + **者** (person who) = follower, adherent, devotee — someone who follows a leader or cause

**八** (bā): Eight

**成** (chéng): Tenth, part, fraction (used in expressing percentages); to succeed, to accomplish; to become, to turn into

**是** (shì): To be, is, am, are, was, were

**裂** (liè): To split, to crack, to tear, to rip apart Contains the **衣** (clothes) radical on the bottom.

**角** (jiǎo / jué): **jiǎo** (third tone): Horn (of an animal), angle, corner, point; **jué** (second tone): role (in theater, as in 角色), to compete

**追** (zhuī): To chase, to pursue, to follow, to seek, to court Contains the **辶** (walk/movement) radical.

**随** (suí): To follow, to comply with, to comply, to let something take its course; as one wishes Contains the **阝** (mound/earth) radical on the left.

**者** (zhě): Person who, one who, those who — a classical suffix forming agent nouns

**干** (gàn / gān): **gàn** (fourth tone): To do, to work, to act; dry, shield, trunk (of a tree); **gān** (first tone): Dry, to dry

**的** (de): Structural particle — in **是…干的**, the **的** forms a nominalized relative clause: "the ones who did it"

**泄露** (xiè lòu): **泄** (to leak out / to vent) + **露** (to reveal / to expose) = to let secret information escape and become known

**泄** (xiè): To leak, to vent, to discharge, to let out, to泄气 (to lose confidence) Contains the **氵** (water) radical on the left.

**露** (lù / lòu): **lù** (fourth tone): Dew, to reveal, to show, to expose; **lòu** (fourth tone): to leak (in collo­quial usage, as in 泄露) Contains the **雨** (rain) radical on top.
